About Us

Welcome to Saravana Traders. We are proud to provide high-quality school bags, stationery products, and festival crackers at affordable prices. Our aim is to make shopping easy by offering reliable products for students, parents, schools, offices, and families.

We have a wide collection of school bags, college bags, travel bags, notebooks, pens, pencils, geometry boxes, water bottles, lunch boxes, files, and many other stationery items. Every product is selected carefully to ensure the best quality.

During the festive season, especially Diwali, we also offer a large variety of premium quality crackers including sparklers, flower pots, rockets, fancy fireworks, gift boxes, and kids' special crackers. We focus on providing branded, safe, and high-quality crackers at reasonable prices.
